Flick Square Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Flick Square is a puzzle game by RV Studios (the trading name of Roan Vasdev), based in New Zealand. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your information. The short version: we don't run accounts or analytics, and the only data collected is by Google's advertising service, and only if you choose to watch an optional video ad.

What we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location or contacts, and the game has no accounts or sign-in. We do not use analytics tools. The only information that leaves your device is what the ad service described below collects.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, levels cleared, hints and undos, and your sound, music, haptics and trail settings) is saved on your device only so the game can remember where you were. We never see it. If you delete the game, it is deleted too.

Optional video ads

You can tap a button in the Store to watch a short video in exchange for a free hint and some undos. Ads are only shown when you choose to watch one. The game has no banner ads and no pop-up ads.

These ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and use your device's advertising identifier, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, device and app information, and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this to deliver and measure ads, to prevent fraud, and, if you allow it, to personalise ads. We receive only a confirmation that the ad was completed so the game can give you your reward.

On iPhone, the game asks for your permission before allowing tracking across other companies' apps and websites (Apple's "App Tracking Transparency" prompt). If you say no, or turn tracking off later in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, ads are still shown but are not personalised using your advertising identifier.

You can read how Google handles this information at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

Purchases

If you buy a hint or undo pack, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details. Apple may share basic purchase confirmation with us, such as that a purchase was made, so the game can deliver what you bought. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that process.

Children

Flick Square is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. We do not collect personal information ourselves; ads are served by Google as described above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this page with a new date. We will update it before any change to how the game handles your information reaches you.
