Flick Square Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Flick Square is a puzzle game by RV Studios (the trading name of Roan Vasdev), based in New Zealand. This policy explains what the game does and does not do with your information. The short version: we don't collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. Flick Square has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising, and it does not send any personal information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your progress (stars, levels cleared, hints and undos, and your sound, haptics and trail settings) is saved on your device only so the game can remember where you were. We never see it. If you delete the game, it is deleted too.

Purchases

If you buy a hint or undo pack, the purchase is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not see or store your payment details. Apple may share basic purchase confirmation with us, such as that a purchase was made, so the game can deliver what you bought. Apple's own privacy policy applies to that process.

Third parties

The game does not include third-party advertising or tracking software. If that ever changes, for example by adding rewarded videos, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy information before the change reaches you.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this page with a new date.
